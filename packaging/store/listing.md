## Description [up to 10,000 characters]
Remember the desktop assistants of the 90s? Pasty is what might have happened if one of them had survived into the modern era: helpful, nostalgic, slightly obnoxious, technically competent, and occasionally sarcastic.

Pasty is an original character: a tube of paste with a screw-cap hat and eyebrows that do a lot of the talking. It lives on your desktop, always on top, wherever you drag it.

WHAT PASTY DOES
• Hangs out on your desktop with idle, listening, thinking and talking animations, and its eyes follow your cursor
• Shares Windows tips, tells (computer) jokes and reacts when you poke, drag or ignore it
• Answers questions when you double-click it, with an optional AI brain
• Slash commands: /joke, /tip, /roll 2d6, /flip, /time and more (type /help)
• A sarcasm slider, from Saintly to Insufferable
• Built to live with all day: naps when you're away, hides during full-screen games and presentations, snoozes on request, and never repeats a joke within the hour

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Pasty collects nothing. No account, no telemetry, no ads. Its personality works completely offline. Optional awareness features (noticing which kind of app you're using, or that you copied something) are off until you turn them on, and what Pasty notices is reduced to a category, such as "code editor" or "a link", then forgotten.

ABOUT THE AI FEATURES
Pasty's built-in brain is not AI: it's a hand-written personality that runs offline. If you want Pasty to answer real questions, you can connect an AI brain in Settings → Brain:
• Ollama, which runs open AI models entirely on your own PC
• Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google) or GitHub Models, using your own API key
• Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint
When an AI brain is on, Pasty's answers to your questions are generated by that AI model and can be wrong; Pasty sends only what you type into its ask box, to the provider you chose. AI brains are off by default, and AI-generated replies appear only in response to your questions. Pasty's own remarks, jokes and tips are always written by humans.

HALLOWEEN EDITION
From October 15 to November 1, Pasty dresses up: a tiny witch hat, fangs, a bat friend, ghost mode (/boo), seasonal jokes and /trickortreat. Spooky, never scary. Turn it off, or keep it on all year.
